# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release for Victoria 3 `1.13.*`.

### 1836 setup

- Redistributes Ottoman population, cultures, and religions across TUR-owned states while keeping the vanilla authored total.
- Corrects selected resource potentials without turning unused deposits into starting industries.
- Rebuilds documented 1836 farms, workshops, ports, and military industry; starting Anatolian tea plantations are removed.
- Reorganizes the four opening armies and keeps the existing fleet; starting commanders and nineteenth-century Ottoman names are corrected.

### Reform and crisis

- Tanzimat objectives now track finances, literacy, secession risk, army composition, and administration instead of a handful of presence checks.
- Sick Man success grants a limited rejuvenation bonus plus one specialization; failure remains severe and no longer kills the ruler.
- Great Eastern Crisis progress, timeout, and failure cleanup use the intended Ottoman/Balkan scopes.
- The Egyptian crisis can close with a limited Levant settlement, a costly maximalist victory, or a stalemate.

### Rumelia, people, and companies

- A Balkan guidance journal explains reform, order, and subject-compromise tools without adding a custom cohesion currency.
- Âlî, Fuad, Ahmed Cevdet, and Nâmık Kemal can appear as historical figures; Fevzi Çakmak, Mustafa Kemal, and Kâzım Karabekir arrive later as distinctive officers.
- Crimean, Circassian, and Rumelian muhacir waves add settlement choices, commissions, and costs rather than free population.
- Şirket-i Hayriye can be founded as a Bosphorus port company; the Imperial Ottoman Bank is offered as a later concession.

### Presentation and compatibility

- English and Turkish localization are complete and kept in parity.
- National Awakening is optional. Community Mod Framework and Community State Framework remain required.
